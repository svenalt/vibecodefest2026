    About this demo

    The iOS prototype recognizes sounds on-device with YAMNet. This browser demo visualizes acoustic features only; it does not classify sounds. Android support is planned.

    AUVIONA is experimental. Do not rely on it for safety, emergencies, navigation or medical decisions.

    02 / THE IDEA

    More than sound recognition.
    A visual language.

    01 / AWARENESS
    ✦

    Awareness, made visible.

    AUVIONA explores how meaningful visual cues can make the sounds around us more accessible — including for people with hearing impairments.

    02 / ART
    ✧

    Science meets art.

    The app’s Universe V2 brings sound to life: flowing ribbons for speech, delicate particles for rain, and glowing forms for music. Expand the artwork into Pure Art or Art + Sounds.

    03 / PRIVACY
    ON
    DEVICE    ⌁

    Private by design.

    In the native iOS prototype, audio recognition happens on the device. No account, cloud audio processing or recording is needed for the live experience.

    03 / BUILT IN THE OPEN

    Open source.
    Open possibilities.

    AUVIONA began as a Vibe Code Fest experiment. Now its source code is available under the MIT License, so anyone can explore it, learn from it, and help make sound more accessible.
