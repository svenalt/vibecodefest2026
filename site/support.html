What AUVIONA does

AUVIONA listens through your iPhone's microphone while the app is open and turns sound into live artwork. It recognises a small set of everyday sounds, shown with a label and icon: speech, music, a barking dog, applause, rain and bells. Very quiet moments show Quiet; anything else shows No confident sound. Everything runs on your iPhone and works offline.

Current version: 1.0.0. Requires an iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later.

Important limitation

AUVIONA is made for art and general sound awareness. It can miss or mislabel sounds. Do not rely on it for emergencies, alarms, safety-critical decisions, navigation or medical decisions.

Troubleshooting

The app says "Microphone blocked"

Microphone access was turned off. Open iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and turn on AUVIONA, or use the Open system settings button in the app. Then return to AUVIONA.

The app is not listening

If you pressed Stop listening, AUVIONA stays off, even after leaving or restarting the app. Tap the gear icon (top right), then Start listening. While listening is on, it pauses whenever you leave the app and resumes when you come back.

A sound is not recognised

Only the sounds listed above are labelled. Bring the iPhone closer to the sound, reduce background noise, and give it a second or two: a sound is shown only after it has been heard consistently.

"Listening stopped" or another problem message

A phone call, another app using audio, or a change of headphones can stop the microphone. Return to the app or press Start listening in Settings. If it keeps happening, contact us with the message shown.

The artwork is still

AUVIONA follows your iPhone's Reduce Motion setting (Settings > Accessibility > Motion). When Reduce Motion is on, the artwork is shown as a still image; turn it off for moving artwork.

Haptic alerts

If available in your version, Settings > Listening > Haptic alerts adds a short vibration when a supported sound is confirmed. It is off by default, is not an alarm, and iOS may mute it (for example with System Haptics turned off).

Privacy

Audio is never recorded, saved or uploaded, and the app collects no data. Read the Privacy Policy.
