AUVIONA

Making sound accessible to all.

Privacy Policy

Effective date of publication. Applies to the AUVIONA app for iPhone, version 1.0 and later.

In short: AUVIONA analyses sound on your iPhone. Audio is never recorded, saved or uploaded. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and makes no network requests. We do not receive any data from the app.

Who is responsible

AUVIONA is provided by operator legal name, postal address, if legally required. Contact: privacy contact email.

Microphone

  • AUVIONA asks for microphone access the first time it starts listening. You can refuse; the app then explains how to allow it later and does not ask again by itself.
  • The microphone is used only while the app is open on screen and listening is on. When you leave the app, it is released. When you press Stop listening, it stays off, even after you return or restart the app, until you press Start listening.
  • While listening, sound is converted into short pieces of audio in your iPhone's memory, analysed by a sound-recognition model built into the app (Google's YAMNet, running on the device), and then discarded. Audio is never written to storage, recorded or sent anywhere.
  • iOS shows its microphone indicator whenever the microphone is in use.

Recognised sounds

The sound labels and the list of recent sounds exist only in the app's memory. They are cleared when listening starts again or the app is closed, and are never stored or shared.

Settings stored on your device

AUVIONA remembers your choices for theme (Dark or Light), whether listening is on, and haptic alerts. These are stored only in the app's local storage on your iPhone. Like other app data, they may be included in your own device backups. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network, analytics and third parties

  • The app makes no network requests and works fully offline. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because the app does not send us any.
  • When you open this page or the support page from the app, it opens in your browser, and the website host may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page: hosting provider and its log retention.
  • Apple processes data when you download the app, and, if you chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings, may provide us with anonymised crash and usage statistics. Apple's privacy policy applies to that.

Your choices

  • Turn listening off in AUVIONA under Settings > Listening.
  • Withdraw microphone access at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone > AUVIONA.
  • Delete the app to remove its stored settings.

Because we hold no personal data from the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You may still contact us with any privacy question or request at privacy contact email. Owner/legal: confirm applicable law (e.g. Swiss FADP, EU GDPR) and supervisory authority wording.

Children

AUVIONA collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version of the app is released.
