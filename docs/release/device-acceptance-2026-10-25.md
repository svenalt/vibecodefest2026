**Status: Accepted by Sven Altermatt (owner-reported physical-device testing).**

Sven confirmed: “REQ-010 ist abgeschlossen” and “Und bitte setze auch alles auf abgenommen. Ich habe alles auf den Geräten getestet.”

REQ-001 through REQ-010 are accepted for the delivered iOS/Android product and device behavior. This includes the simplified Settings and persistent immersive Art + Sounds display in REQ-010, local recognition, listening controls, appearance/accessibility and haptics. Earlier UI requirements superseded by later REQs remain historical specifications, not requests to restore removed features.

This is owner acceptance, not a claim that the assistant repeated the tests. Exact device models, OS versions, build IDs, measurements, screenshots and scenario-level logs were not supplied in this confirmation and are not invented here. Historical “not run on device” statements describe the earlier coding environment and are superseded as the current product acceptance status.

The three newly reproduced edge cases and pending identifier/link configuration are tracked in [REQ-011](../requirements/REQ-011-auviona-audio-recovery-release-finalization.md). REQ-011 is ready for implementation and is not already accepted. Privacy/Support pages exist according to Sven; wiring their exact URLs into the app remains part of REQ-011.

This acceptance does not assert an independent iOS binary telemetry/privacy audit, a signed archive upload, a TestFlight build identifier, legal clearance or completed App Store submission. Those require their own evidence and are separate from acceptance of tested product behavior.
